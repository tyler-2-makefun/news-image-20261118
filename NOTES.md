# NOTES — News research for 18 November 2026

Researched 2026-11-20 (WIB) via web search. All six stories below were
reported on 18 November 2026 by at least two independent sources. Nothing
was invented; where reporting was cautious (e.g. the Kerch Bridge damage),
the image wording says "claims" rather than stating it as fact.

## 1. Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen (DIPLOMACY)
- Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wed 18 Nov, one day
  before the first-ever trilateral summit with US President Donald Trump.
- Agenda: Ukraine, Iran, nuclear/strategic stability, trade.
- Sources: AFP / AP, as reported by Open Chronicle
  (https://openchronicle.org/2026/11/18/global-news-brief-key-developments-around-the-world-november-18-2026/);
  Reuters, "Trump's historic meeting with Xi, Putin headlines Asia trip"
  (https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trumps-historic-meeting-with-xi-putin-headlines-asia-trip-2026-11-18/).

## 2. Iran strikes Kuwait and Bahrain (MIDDLE EAST)
- After the US hit a missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran struck at Kuwait
  and Bahrain; Bahrain said its air defences intercepted Iranian aerial
  attacks, Kuwait said it was countering hostile drones. Oil near $93.
- Sources: "Daily News — November 18, 2026" (YouTube, 18 Nov 2026);
  PaisaKawach, "Today's Top 3" 18 Nov 2026, compiled from Al Jazeera,
  France 24, Business Recorder, CNN and The Hindu
  (https://paisakawach.com/news/gulf-attacks-nvidia-huang-cyclone-akvan-top-news-18-november-2026).

## 3. Kerch Bridge hit for a second day (UKRAINE)
- Ukraine claims a major strike on the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge, the second
  day running; part of the road span collapsed. Independent verification
  of the exact structural damage was still pending at the time, so the
  image says "Ukraine claims major damage".
- Sources: "Daily News — November 18, 2026" (YouTube, 18 Nov 2026);
  Open Chronicle 18 Nov 2026 brief ("Ukraine reports major damage to the
  Crimean Bridge", noting verification still needed).

## 4. Peltola wins Alaska Senate seat (UNITED STATES)
- Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in
  Alaska, giving Democrats a projected 56-44 majority in the incoming
  Senate.
- Sources: The Associated Press and Alaska Public Media, as reported by
  Open Chronicle 18 Nov 2026; "Daily News — November 18, 2026" (YouTube).

## 5. Cyclone Akvan forces mass evacuations (INDIA)
- Severe cyclonic storm Akvan approached India's east coast; more than
  47,000 people evacuated in Odisha, schools shut in Malkangiri, South
  Odisha on high alert.
- Sources: BilkulOnline "Daily News Wrap" 18-19 Nov 2026
  (https://bilkulonline.com/2026/11/19/daily-news-wrap-bilkulonline-138/);
  PaisaKawach 18 Nov 2026.

## 6. Nvidia board rejects Huang's return (TECH)
- Nvidia's board unanimously accepted Jensen Huang's resignation and said
  it does not intend to reinstate him as CEO, even as the company posted
  a record $115.8B quarter.
- Sources: "Daily News — November 18, 2026" (YouTube, 18 Nov 2026);
  PaisaKawach 18 Nov 2026.

## Stories considered and NOT used
- Ebola outbreak in DR Congo passing 11,000 cases and the US losing
  measles-free status: appeared only in the single Daily News roundup
  without a second corroborating source in the time available, so they
  were left out rather than risk an unverified claim.
- APEC Shenzhen summit opening: real, but covered by story 1 (same
  Shenzhen diplomacy cluster) to avoid duplication.
